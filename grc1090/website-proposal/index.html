Introduction

Rice Rice Baby

My company is called Rice Rice Baby, it's a modern Persian food restaurant that prioritizes the taste of home and brings the customer back to their family home, eating Persian food with the people they love. The restaurant will have a communal seating style, with multiple long tables instead of individual ones.

Target audience

My target audience is young adults who are looking for homecooked Persian food away from home, or those who want to experience and learn more about Persian culture!

Their primary tasks and goals are to have a nice meal, spend time with friends and family in a homey environment, and eat good food!

About Us

Our restaurant uses recipes from families all over the world, with each one being a little bit different (feel free to send us your family recipes!) Our restaurant has a communal style layout, which means that instead of individual tables everyone sits together, emphasizing the hope of creating a community if you're away from home or learning about our culture if you're curious!
