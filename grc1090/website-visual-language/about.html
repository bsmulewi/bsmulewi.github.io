Welcome to Rice Rice Baby, a family-owned Persian restaurant inspired by the flavors, traditions, and warmth of home. Our food is made to bring people together around the table—whether you’re discovering Persian cuisine for the first time or enjoying a meal that feels familiar.

Persian food is all about balance: fragrant rice, tender grilled meats, bright herbs, rich stews, and comforting spices. We honor those classic flavors while bringing them to life in a fresh, modern setting.

At Rice Rice Baby, every meal is meant to feel welcoming. We want our restaurant to be a place where friends catch up, families celebrate, and everyone can slow down and share something delicious.

From our kitchen to your table, we’re proud to serve food made with care, culture, and plenty of love. Come hungry, bring the people you love, and let us welcome you into the Rice Rice Baby family.
